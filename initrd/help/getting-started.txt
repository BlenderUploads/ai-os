Getting started
===============

HALCYON boots to a desktop. Windows work the way you expect: drag the title
bar to move, drag any edge or corner to resize, double-click the title bar to
maximise.

Keyboard
  F1 - F10        open applications
  alt+tab         cycle windows (add shift to go backwards)
  ctrl+W          close the focused window
  ctrl+M          minimise it
  alt+up          maximise, alt+down restores or minimises
  alt+left/right  snap to half the screen
  F11             fullscreen, and F11 again to come back

Mouse
  right-click the desktop    the launcher, plus tile and cascade
  right-click a title bar    that window's menu
  right-click the taskbar    the launcher and display settings
  drag a window to an edge   snap it there

Everything on this screen was drawn by code in this repository: the font, the
window frames, the pointer, the scanlines.
