# Open Settings and change the screen resolution while the machine runs.
#
# The interesting assertion is in the serial log: RESOLUTION-CHANGED only
# appears once the compositor has rebuilt itself around the new geometry, and
# the screenshots that follow it are captured at the new size.

wait 1.5
keys ctrl-w
wait 0.4
keys ctrl-w
wait 0.8
# F6 is Settings.
keys f6
wait 1.5
shot settings-display
# Down one from the top of the list, then apply it.
keys down
wait 0.5
keys ret
wait 3
shot settings-after-mode-change
# Back down to a smaller one, to prove it goes both ways.
keys down down down down down down
wait 0.5
keys ret
wait 3
shot settings-smaller
keys tab
wait 1
shot settings-sound
# The test tone doubles as a check that audio survived the mode change.
keys down down down down down
wait 0.4
keys ret
wait 2
keys tab
wait 1
shot settings-system
